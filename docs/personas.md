# Buyer personas

These personas guide later AI-buyer and demo work. Phase 0 only supports browsing the seeded catalog.

## 1. Ananya — budget student laptop

- **Need:** A laptop for lectures, notes, and light coding.
- **Constraints:** Budget ₹80,000. Wants 16 GB RAM. Must be in stock before semester starts.
- **Trade-off:** Prefers a lighter 14-inch machine over a cheaper 15-inch with 8 GB RAM.
- **Phase 0 action:** Open the product grid and compare AuraBook 14 vs Nexus Lite 15 vs Peak Pro 16 on the detail pages.

## 2. Vikram — parent buying a gift

- **Need:** Over-ear headphones as a birthday gift.
- **Constraints:** Budget around ₹15,000. Prefers noise cancellation. Will not buy out-of-stock items.
- **Trade-off:** Willing to skip cheaper earbuds if the over-ear pair is clearly better for travel.
- **Phase 0 action:** Open Sonic Over-Ear Headphones and Pulse In-Ear Earbuds and compare price, stock, and specs.

## 3. Meera — home-office professional

- **Need:** A desk monitor that works with her existing laptop.
- **Constraints:** USB-C preferred. Rating 4.5 or higher. In stock this week. Budget ₹20,000.
- **Trade-off:** Will add a keyboard or mouse later; for now she wants one primary product she can confirm herself.
- **Phase 0 action:** Open ClearView 27-inch Monitor and confirm trusted price and stock on the detail page.
